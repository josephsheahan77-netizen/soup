# Gorge Bowl: one page for restaurants and sponsors

**Gorge Bowl is a free app that shows what soup every lunch spot in the Gorge has today.**
No reviews, no ratings, no comments. Just "what's in the pot today, and where."
Open it at **localbowl.com/gorge**, or scan the QR code.

> Some days you just want a good cup of soup. Some days you just need to dance.
> Here's the place to find out where.

---

## For restaurants: free

**What you get**
- Your soup and daily special in front of everyone deciding where to eat lunch today.
- A **"Posted by the restaurant"** tag. Once you post, your soup is locked for the day, so
  nobody else can change it.
- A map link straight to your door.
- A fair spot: the list reshuffles randomly every day. Nobody can pay to be on top, including you.

**What it takes from you**
- Each morning you get a short reminder: *"What's the soup today?"*
- Reply with the soup ("French onion, Reuben special"). About 10 seconds. That's it.
- If you forget, your regulars can still post it for you, and you can fix it any time.

**What we need from you, once**
- One email or phone number for the reminder and to mark you as the real restaurant.
- That's all we keep. It stays private and isn't shared or sold. Once a year we might ask if you'd
  like to chip in as a sponsor. No pressure.

---

## For sponsors: small, classy, local

No pop-ups, no sound, nothing that gets in the way of the soup. Two ways to be part of it:

**Season sponsor: one business per season, $250**
- "Spring on Gorge Bowl is brought to you by ___." Your name at the top of the app all season.
- Your colors and logo in the sponsor strip, made to fit the season.
- Seasons follow the calendar: winter (soup season), spring, summer, fall. Tie it to your
  season: fresh hop for a brewery, cherry season for an orchard, ski season for a shop.

**Monthly banner: up to 5 businesses, $50 a month**
- A small banner at the bottom of the app, shared fairly. The order is random and switches every 20 seconds.
- Tap it and it opens your website, menu, or map.
- Your space is 640 × 100 pixels. Bring something fun. (Specs: SPONSORS.md)

Being a sponsor never moves your soup in the list. Sponsors make the app possible; the list stays fair.

## Questions people ask

**Isn't this just Facebook?** No. You don't need Facebook or Instagram to look or to post, and there's
nothing to argue about: no comments, no reviews.

**What if someone posts something mean or fake?** Mean words are blocked, the restaurant's own post
always wins, and anything flagged gets hidden until it's checked.

**Who runs it?** A local, Joe. Ask him anything.
