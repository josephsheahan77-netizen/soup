Daily soup reminder

A nudge at 9:30 each morning to post your soup on Gorge Bowl.

Are you a restaurant? Set yourself a daily reminder

Tap the button for your phone, then tap Add or Save.

iPhone: add to my calendar Android: add to Google Calendar
To stop it later, delete the event from your calendar. Rather not use the calendar? Set a daily alarm in your Clock app instead.

Same soup for a few days?

When you post, pick Same soup for: 2, 3, 4 or 5 days. It stays up and won't reset tonight, so you don't have to post again until it changes.
