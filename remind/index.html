Daily soup reminder

A nudge at 9:30 each morning to post your soup on Gorge Bowl.

Tap the button for your phone, then tap Add or Save.

iPhone: add to my calendar Android: add to Google Calendar Want a different time? Change it in your calendar after adding. Want it to stop? Delete the event from your calendar. Rather not use the calendar? Set a daily 9:30 alarm in your Clock app instead.
