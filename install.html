Add Gorge Bowl to your phone

One tap from your home screen, like any other app.

iPhone

  1. Open josephsheahan77-netizen.github.io/soup in Safari (or scan the Gorge Bowl QR code with your camera).
  2. Tap the Share button. On newer iPhones, tap ••• first, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top corner.

Don't see Add to Home Screen? Make sure you're in Safari, then scroll the list all the way down or tap Edit Actions to find it.

Android

  1. Open josephsheahan77-netizen.github.io/soup in Chrome (or scan the Gorge Bowl QR code with your camera).
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (some phones say Install app).
  4. Tap Install or Add.

Using Samsung Internet? Tap the ≡ menu, then Add page to → Home screen.

That's it

Look for the soup bowl icon on your home screen. Tap it any day to see what's in the soup pots around the Gorge.
